You do not need a new plan and you are not charged again. Your LedgerSync plan covers it. New clients
you add through the AI count on your plan, just like clients you add in the app.

**Do not create a new account and do not pay.** You sign in with the same username and password you use in
the LedgerSync app.

<Note>
  For now, only the firm owner can connect LedgerSync. Access for teammates is coming.
</Note>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add LedgerSync to your AI">
    Follow [Set up in Claude](/ai/setup-claude) or [Set up in ChatGPT](/ai/setup-chatgpt).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with your LedgerSync login">
    The LedgerSync sign-in page opens. Type your email or your LedgerSync username. Click **Next**.
    Already signed in to LedgerSync in this browser? You go straight to the **Allow** page.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/connect-1-email.png" alt="The Sign in to LedgerSync page with the Next button marked" />
    </Frame>

    Type your LedgerSync password. Click **Sign in**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/connect-2-password.png" alt="The Enter your password page with the Sign in button marked" />
    </Frame>

    See **Create your LedgerSync account**? Do not click it. Click **Change** and type your LedgerSync
    username.

    You use two-step sign-in? Type your code and click **Verify**. Forgot your password? Click
    **Forgot password?**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Allow">
    You see your firm's name and your name. Click **Allow**. Then your AI asks to access your account: click
    **Allow access**. You only see that the first time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your clients are already there. Ask your first question.
