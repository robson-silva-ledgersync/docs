<AiStep id="sign-in" n={2} total={3} title="Sign in with your LedgerSync login" img="/images/ai-connector/full/ls-2-password.png" caption="Sign in circled">

Type the email or username you use in the LedgerSync app. Click **Next**. Type your password. Click
**Sign in**.

You use two-step sign-in? Type your code and click **Verify**. Forgot your password? Click
**Forgot password?**.

<AiNote tone="yellow">

See **Create your LedgerSync account**? Don't click it. Click **Change** and type your LedgerSync username.

</AiNote>

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="allow" n={3} total={3} title="Click Allow" img="/images/ai-connector/full/ls-5-allow.png" caption="Allow circled">

You see your firm's name and your name. Click **Allow**. Then your AI asks to access your account: click
**Allow access**. You only see that the first time.

That's it. Your clients are already there.

</AiStep>

<AiNote tone="gray">

**Only the firm owner, for now.** Only the firm owner can connect LedgerSync. Access for teammates is
coming. New clients you add through the AI count on your plan, just like clients you add in the app.

</AiNote>
