## Most common problems

| The AI says | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **We're reviewing your new firm. You can add clients now. Bank connections open after the review.** | Nothing to fix. Add your clients now. Bank connections open after our review. |
| **LedgerSync is not set up for this sign-in yet.** Or: **This LedgerSync connection is not active.** | [Remove LedgerSync from your AI](#remove) and add it again. On the LedgerSync page, type your email: it signs you in, or helps you create your account. Still not working? Email support. |
| **Your LedgerSync password was changed, so this connection was turned off.** | [Remove LedgerSync from your AI](#remove) and add it again. Sign in again, with your new password if you changed it. |
| **For now, only the firm owner can use LedgerSync in their own Claude or ChatGPT.** | Access for teammates is coming. For now, ask your firm owner. |
| It doesn't seem to know LedgerSync | Start a new chat and ask again. In Claude, click **+** in the message box, then **Connectors**, and turn **LedgerSync** on. In ChatGPT, type **@** and pick **LedgerSync**. |

## Remove or turn off LedgerSync

<div className="lsai-grid-2">
  <AiBox id="remove" title="Remove it from your AI">

  **Claude:** open **Customize**, then **Connectors**, and click **LedgerSync**. Open its **⋮** menu and click
  **Remove**.

  **Claude Team or Enterprise member:** click **Disconnect** instead. To use it again, click **Connect** and
  sign in, as in [step 4 of Set up in Claude](/ai/setup-claude#sign-in). Only your firm's Claude Owner can
  remove it for everyone, under **Organization settings**, then **Connectors**.

  **ChatGPT:** open **Settings**, then **Plugins**, and click **LedgerSync**. Open the **...** menu and click
  **Disconnect**. To use it again, click **LedgerSync** there and click **Connect**. Then do
  [step 4 of Set up in ChatGPT](/ai/setup-chatgpt#sign-in).

  </AiBox>
  <AiBox id="turn-off" title="Turn it off for your firm">

  Change your LedgerSync password. Every AI you connected with your LedgerSync app login stops working within
  a minute. To use LedgerSync in your AI again, add it again and sign in with your new password.

  Connected through the developer portal (a developer account, or an AI app connected before October 1,
  2026)? Open [portal.ledgersyncappv2.com](https://portal.ledgersyncappv2.com) on a computer. See **Sandbox**
  at the top? Pick **Live** first. Click **Connected apps**, then **Disconnect** next to each app. It loses
  access at once.

  To stop it for good, email [support@ledgersync.com](mailto:support@ledgersync.com) and we will turn it off.

  </AiBox>
</div>
