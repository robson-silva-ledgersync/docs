## Most common problems

| The AI says | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **We're reviewing your new firm. You can add clients now. Bank connections open after the review.** | Nothing to fix. Add your clients now: ask "Add my client" and the client's name. Bank connections open after our review. |
| **LedgerSync is not set up for this sign-in yet.** Or: **This LedgerSync connection is not active.** | [Connect again](#connect-again). Sign in with the username or email you use in the LedgerSync app. Don't create a second account. Still not working? Email <AiEmail />. |
| **Your LedgerSync password was changed, so this connection was turned off.** | [Connect again](#connect-again). Sign in again, with your new password if you changed it. |
| **For now, only the firm owner can use LedgerSync in their own Claude or ChatGPT.** | Teammates can connect now. [Connect again](#connect-again) and sign in with your own LedgerSync login. |
| **This LedgerSync login cannot use LedgerSync in Claude or ChatGPT.** | [Connect again](#connect-again) and sign in with your own LedgerSync login. Still there? Email <AiEmail />. |
| It asks you to disconnect and reconnect LedgerSync, or it doesn't know something new, like check images links | Your AI still has an older copy of LedgerSync's tools. Do [After a LedgerSync update](#after-an-update). |
| It doesn't seem to know LedgerSync | Start a new chat and ask again. In Claude, click **+** in the message box, then **Connectors**, and turn **LedgerSync** on. In ChatGPT, type **@** and pick **LedgerSync**. Not in the list? Open [Plugins](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), click **Personal**, then **LedgerSync**, then **Install plugin**. |

<AiBox id="connect-again" title="To connect again">

**Claude:** open [Customize, then Connectors, then Yours](https://claude.ai/customize/connectors/yours) and click
**LedgerSync**. Click **Disconnect** and confirm, if you see it. Then click **Connect**.

**ChatGPT:** open [Settings, then Plugins](https://chatgpt.com/settings/plugins-settings) and click **LedgerSync**. Click
**...** next to your account, then **Reconnect**.

The LedgerSync sign-in page opens. Sign in, then click **Allow**.

</AiBox>

<AiBox id="after-an-update" title="After a LedgerSync update">

Claude and ChatGPT keep a copy of LedgerSync's tools. When we add something new, load the new copy once:

**Claude:** [connect again](#connect-again).

**ChatGPT:** open [Plugins](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), click **Personal**, then **LedgerSync**, then
**Refresh**.

Then start a new chat.

</AiBox>

## Remove or turn off LedgerSync

<div className="lsai-grid-2">
  <AiBox id="remove" title="Remove it from your AI" img="/images/ai-connector/full/claude-remove.png" caption="Remove circled (Claude)">

  **Claude:** [Customize, then Connectors, then Yours](https://claude.ai/customize/connectors/yours), then **LedgerSync**, then
  the **⋮** menu, then **Remove**. On Claude Team or Enterprise, click **Disconnect** instead.

  **ChatGPT:** [Settings, then Plugins](https://chatgpt.com/settings/plugins-settings), then **LedgerSync**,
  then **Uninstall**. To remove it completely, also open [Plugins](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), click **Personal**,
  then **LedgerSync**, then the **...** menu, then **Delete plugin**.

  </AiBox>
  <AiBox id="turn-off" title="Turn it off">

  **For yourself:** Change your LedgerSync password. Every AI you connected with your LedgerSync login stops
  working within a minute. To use LedgerSync in your AI again, [connect again](#connect-again) and sign in with
  your new password.

  **For a teammate (firm owner):** Email <AiEmail /> and we will turn it off for that person. Removing the person
  from your Employees or Administrators page in LedgerSync also stops it within a minute. Your own password
  change does not disconnect teammates.

  **For the whole firm:** Email <AiEmail /> and we will turn it off for everyone at your firm.

  </AiBox>
</div>
