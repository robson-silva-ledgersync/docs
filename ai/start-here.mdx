<AiNote tone="gray">

**Good to know.** Claude calls this a **connector**. ChatGPT calls it an **app** or **plugin**. They are the
same thing.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="green">

**Nobody types a bank password in the chat.** When a bank needs a password, the AI gives you a link to a
LedgerSync page. That page opens your bank's secure sign-in. The password goes there.

</AiNote>
