<AiNote tone="green">

**Sign in with your normal LedgerSync login.** The firm owner, administrators and employees each sign in with
their own login and see what they see in LedgerSync. Please don't create a second account.

</AiNote>

Your firm has no LedgerSync account yet? Sign up at [ledgersyncappv2.com/sign-up](https://ledgersyncappv2.com/sign-up) first, then come back here.

## Which AI do you use?

<div className="lsai-grid-2">
  <AiChoice title="Claude" href="/ai/setup-claude" cta="Set up in Claude →">
    Works on any Claude plan. On Claude Team or Enterprise, your firm's Claude Owner adds LedgerSync first.
  </AiChoice>
  <AiChoice title="ChatGPT" href="/ai/setup-chatgpt" cta="Set up in ChatGPT →">
    Needs ChatGPT Plus or higher, on chatgpt.com in a web browser. On ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Edu, your workspace admin adds LedgerSync first.
  </AiChoice>
</div>

<AiNote tone="gray">

**Good to know.** Claude calls this a **connector**. ChatGPT calls it an **app** or **plugin**. They are the
same thing.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="green">

**Your clients' bank passwords are never typed in the chat.** When a bank needs a password, the AI gives you a
link to a LedgerSync page. The sign-in happens there, or on our bank-data partner's secure page that it opens.

</AiNote>

Developer building on the LedgerSync API? Go to [For developers](/ai/developers).
