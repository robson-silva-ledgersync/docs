## Which AI do you use?

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Set up in Claude" icon="message" href="/ai/setup-claude">
    Works on every Claude plan. The Free plan allows only one added connector. On Claude Team or
    Enterprise, your firm's Claude Owner adds LedgerSync first.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Set up in ChatGPT" icon="comments" href="/ai/setup-chatgpt">
    Needs ChatGPT Plus or higher, on chatgpt.com in a web browser. On ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Edu,
    only your workspace admin can add it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  **Good to know.** Claude calls this a connector. ChatGPT calls it an app or plugin. They are the same thing.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Nobody types a bank password in the chat.** When a bank needs a password, the AI gives you a link to a
  LedgerSync page. That page opens your bank's secure sign-in. The password goes there.
</Note>
