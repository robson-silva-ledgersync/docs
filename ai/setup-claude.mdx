  <AiCopy label="Your LedgerSync address" text="https://api.ledgersyncappv2.com/mcp" />
  <AiNote tone="gray">

  **Claude Team or Enterprise?** Your firm's Claude Owner adds LedgerSync first: **Organization settings**,
  then **Connectors**, then **Add**, then **Custom**, then **Web**. Then open **Connectors**, find
  **LedgerSync**, click **Connect** and go to step 4. **Claude Free plan?** It allows only one added
  connector: remove the other one first.

  </AiNote>
</div>

<AiStep id="add" n={1} total={5} title="Click Add LedgerSync to Claude" img="/images/ai-connector/full/claude-1-add-box.png" caption="Name and address filled in">

<AiButton href="https://claude.ai/customize/connectors?modal=add-custom-connector&connectorName=LedgerSync&connectorUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fapi.ledgersyncappv2.com%2Fmcp">Add LedgerSync to Claude</AiButton>

Claude opens a box with **LedgerSync** and the address already filled in. It also shows a red note: **This
connector was suggested by an external link**. That is expected. Check that the address is
`https://api.ledgersyncappv2.com/mcp`.

LedgerSync is already under **Connectors** and says **Connected**? Skip to step 5.

<Accordion title="The button did not work? Add it by hand">
  Copy the address above. In Claude, open **Customize**, then **Connectors**. Click **Add**, then **Add
  custom connector**.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/ai-connector/claude-2-add-custom-connector.png" alt="The Add button and the Add custom connector option marked" />
  </Frame>

  Type **LedgerSync** in the name box. Click the address box, then press **Ctrl+V** (Windows) or
  **Command+V** (Mac). Then do step 2.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/ai-connector/claude-3-name-and-address.png" alt="The name LedgerSync and the address typed in" />
  </Frame>
</Accordion>

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="click-add" n={2} total={5} title="Click Continue, then Add" img="/images/ai-connector/full/claude-2-add.png" caption="Add circled">

Click **Continue**. On the next screen, don't change anything. Click **Add**.

Claude says **A connector with this URL already exists in your organization**? LedgerSync is already added.
Open **Customize**, then **Connectors**, click **LedgerSync**, and go to step 3.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="connect" n={3} total={5} title="Click Connect" img="/images/ai-connector/full/claude-3-connect.png" caption="Connect circled">

Click **Connect**. The LedgerSync sign-in page opens.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="sign-in" n={4} total={5} title="Sign in, then click Allow" img="/images/ai-connector/full/claude-4-allow-access.png" caption="Allow access circled">

Sign in with your LedgerSync email or username, and your password. Click **Allow**. Then Claude asks to
access your account: click **Allow access**. You only see this the first time.

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**New to LedgerSync?** You create your account on this sign-in page. See
[New to LedgerSync](/ai/new-to-ledgersync).

</AiNote>

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="always-allow" n={5} total={5} title="Stop Claude asking permission every time" img="/images/ai-connector/full/claude-5-always-allow.png" caption="Always allow circled">

Open **Customize**, then **Connectors**, and click **LedgerSync**. Next to **Read-only tools**, pick
**Always allow**. Claude still asks before it changes anything: for changes, click **Allow once**.

</AiStep>

<AiTest text="Use LedgerSync to check whether my account is ready.">

Click **New** and paste it. Claude doesn't use LedgerSync? Click **+** in the message box, then
**Connectors**, and turn **LedgerSync** on. LedgerSync now also works in the Claude desktop and phone apps.

</AiTest>
