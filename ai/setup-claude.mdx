New to LedgerSync? You create your account when you sign in, in the steps below.

Use [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) on a computer.

<Note>
  **Claude Free plan?** It allows only one added connector. Already added another one? Remove it first.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Claude Team or Enterprise?** Your firm's Claude Owner adds LedgerSync first: **Organization settings**,
  then **Connectors**, then **Add**, then **Custom**, then **Web**. After that you do not add anything: open
  **Connectors**, find **LedgerSync**, click **Connect**, and go to [step 4](#sign-in).
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check whether LedgerSync is already there" id="check">
    Open [claude.ai](https://claude.ai). Click **Customize**, then **Connectors**. No menu on the left? Click
    the sidebar button at the top left.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/claude-1-customize-connectors.png" alt="Claude menu with Customize and the Connectors tab marked" />
    </Frame>

    Click **Yours**. Is LedgerSync already there?

    - It says **Connected**: go to [step 5](#always-allow).
    - It says **Connect** or **Reconnect**: click it and go to [step 4](#sign-in).
    - It is not there: do the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Add to Claude button" id="add">
    <Card title="Add to Claude" icon="plus" href="https://claude.ai/customize/connectors?modal=add-custom-connector&connectorName=LedgerSync&connectorUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fapi.ledgersyncappv2.com%2Fmcp">
      Opens Claude with LedgerSync already filled in.
    </Card>

    Claude opens the **Add custom connector** box. The name **LedgerSync** and the address are already
    filled in.

    Claude also shows a red box: **This connector was suggested by an external link. Verify the name and
    URL are from a developer you trust before adding.** That is expected. Check that the address is
    `https://api.ledgersyncappv2.com/mcp`. Then click **Continue**.

    Your box shows **Add** instead of **Continue**? Do not change anything. Click **Add** and go to
    [step 4](#sign-in).

    <Accordion title="The button did not work? Add it by hand">
      Copy this address. Click the copy icon on the right of the box.

      ```text
      https://api.ledgersyncappv2.com/mcp
      ```

      In **Connectors**, click **Add**, then **Add custom connector**.

      <Frame>
        <img src="/images/ai-connector/claude-2-add-custom-connector.png" alt="The Add button and the Add custom connector option marked" />
      </Frame>

      Type **LedgerSync** in the name box. Click the address box, then press **Ctrl+V** (Windows) or
      **Command+V** (Mac). Click **Continue**.

      <Frame>
        <img src="/images/ai-connector/claude-3-name-and-address.png" alt="The name LedgerSync and the address typed in, with Continue marked" />
      </Frame>

      Then do the next step.
    </Accordion>

    Claude says this address is already added? Look under **Yours** in [step 1](#check). Not there? Reload
    the page, or sign out of Claude and back in. Then try again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add" id="click-add">
    Do not change anything on this screen. Click **Add**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/claude-4-add.png" alt="The second screen with the Add button marked" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect and sign in to LedgerSync" id="sign-in">
    Click **Connect** (skip this if you already clicked it).

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/claude-5-connect.png" alt="LedgerSync connector page with the Connect button marked" />
    </Frame>

    The LedgerSync sign-in page opens. Type your email or your LedgerSync username. Click **Next**.
    Already signed in to LedgerSync in this browser? You go straight to the **Allow** page.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/connect-1-email.png" alt="The Sign in to LedgerSync page with the Next button marked" />
    </Frame>

    - **Your firm uses LedgerSync:** type your LedgerSync password. Click **Sign in**. You use two-step
      sign-in? Type your code and click **Verify**. See **Create your LedgerSync account** instead? Do not
      click it. Click **Change** and type your LedgerSync username.
    - **New to LedgerSync:** the page says **Create your LedgerSync account**. Click **Create account** and
      follow [New to LedgerSync](/ai/new-to-ledgersync). It ends on the **Allow** page below.
    - **Approved developer accounts:** type your developer portal email. LedgerSync sends you to the developer
      portal sign-in. An application still waiting for its first approval cannot connect yet.

    Then click **Allow**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/connect-5-allow.png" alt="The Connect your AI assistant page with the Allow button marked" />
    </Frame>

    Claude asks to access your account. Click **Allow access**. You only see this the first time.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/ledgersync-allow-access.png" alt="LedgerSync page asking to let Claude access your account, with Allow access marked" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Claude read without asking each time" id="always-allow">
    In **Customize**, then **Connectors**, click **LedgerSync**. Next to **Read-only tools**, pick **Always
    allow**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/claude-6-always-allow-reading.png" alt="Read-only tools menu with Always allow marked" />
    </Frame>

    Claude keeps asking before it changes anything, unless you click **Always allow** on one of those
    questions. For changes, click **Allow once**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it in a new chat" id="test">
    Click **New**. Ask:

    ```text
    Use LedgerSync to check whether my account is ready.
    ```

    Claude does not use LedgerSync? Click **+** in the message box, then **Connectors**, and turn
    **LedgerSync** on.

    LedgerSync now also works in the Claude desktop and phone apps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Now ask

<AiAsk />

To remove LedgerSync from Claude, see [Troubleshooting](/ai/troubleshooting#remove-or-turn-off-ledgersync).

Stuck? Email [support@ledgersync.com](mailto:support@ledgersync.com) with the step and what the screen says.
Never send passwords or card numbers.
