<AiPage title="Set up in Claude" subtitle="5 steps · about 3 minutes · on claude.ai in a web browser" video="/videos/setup-claude.mp4" poster="/videos/setup-claude-poster.jpg" stage={2}>

<Accordion title="On Claude Team or Enterprise? Open this first">

Your firm's Claude **Owner** adds LedgerSync to your firm's Claude once. Then your firm's LedgerSync owner connects
it. Skip steps 1 and 2 below. Not the Claude Owner? Send them this page.

**The Claude Owner, once:**

- Open [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and check that the bottom left shows your firm's account. If it doesn't,
  click your name or initials there and pick your firm. Then come back to this tab.
- Click the button below. Claude opens in a new tab with **LedgerSync** and the address already filled in. A red
  note about an external link is fine. Change nothing, and keep **Use Claude's published identity** picked if you
  see it. Click **Continue** each time you see it, then click **Add**. Don't see **Add**? Scroll down inside
  Claude's box. Then come back to this tab.

<AiButton href="https://claude.ai/admin-settings/connectors?modal=add-custom-connector&connectorName=LedgerSync&connectorUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fapi.ledgersyncappv2.com%2Fmcp" newTab>Add LedgerSync for your firm</AiButton>

**The button did not work?** Click **Copy** below first. Then open
[claude.ai/admin-settings/connectors](https://claude.ai/admin-settings/connectors) and click **Add**, then
**Custom**, then **Web**. Type **LedgerSync** in the name box. Click the address box and press **Ctrl+V**
(**Command+V** on a Mac). Then click **Continue** and **Add** as above.

<AiCopy label="Your LedgerSync address" text="https://api.ledgersyncappv2.com/mcp" />

- Everyone in your firm's Claude now sees LedgerSync. Tell your team: for now only your firm's LedgerSync owner
  connects it, and nobody creates a new LedgerSync account.

**Then your firm's LedgerSync owner (it may be the same person):**

- Check that the bottom left of Claude shows your firm's account (click your name or initials to switch).
- Open [claude.ai/customize/connectors/yours](https://claude.ai/customize/connectors/yours). Click **LedgerSync**
  (it may say **Custom**), then **Connect**. Not in that list? Type **LedgerSync** in
  **Search connectors**.
- Then do [Step 3](#sign-in), Step 4 and Step 5 below, and the test.

</Accordion>

<AiStep id="add" stage={2} n={1} total={5} title="Click Add LedgerSync to Claude" img="/images/ai-connector/full/claude-1-add-box.png" caption="Name, address and Continue circled">

<AiButton href="https://claude.ai/customize/connectors?modal=add-custom-connector&connectorName=LedgerSync&connectorUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fapi.ledgersyncappv2.com%2Fmcp" newTab>Add LedgerSync to Claude</AiButton>

Claude opens in a new tab, with **LedgerSync** and the address already filled in. It also shows a red note:
**This connector was suggested by an external link**. That is expected. Come back to this tab for step 2.

LedgerSync is already in your Claude connectors and says **Connected**? Go to step 5.

<Accordion title="The button did not work? Add it by hand">

<AiCopy label="Your LedgerSync address" text="https://api.ledgersyncappv2.com/mcp" />

Click **Copy**. Open [claude.ai/customize/connectors](https://claude.ai/customize/connectors). Click **Add**,
then **Add custom connector**. Type **LedgerSync** in the name box. Click the address box and press **Ctrl+V**
(Windows) or **Command+V** (Mac). Then do step 2.

</Accordion>

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="connect" stage={2} n={2} total={5} title="Click Continue, then Add, then Connect" imgs={[{ src: "/images/ai-connector/full/claude-2-add.png", caption: "Add circled" }, { src: "/images/ai-connector/full/claude-3-connect.png", caption: "Connect circled" }]}>

Click **Continue** at the bottom right of the box.

The next box has technical words. Don't change anything: scroll down inside the box and click **Add**.

Then click **Connect**. The LedgerSync sign-in page opens.

<Accordion title="Claude shows a message instead?">

**A connector with this URL already exists in your organization**: LedgerSync is already added. Open
[claude.ai/customize/connectors/yours](https://claude.ai/customize/connectors/yours), click **LedgerSync**, then
**Connect**.

On the Claude Free plan you can add only one connector. Remove the other one first.

</Accordion>

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="sign-in" stage={3} n={3} total={5} title="Sign in with your LedgerSync login" imgs={[{ src: "/images/ai-connector/full/ls-1-email.png", caption: "Your email or username, then Next" }, { src: "/images/ai-connector/full/ls-2-password.png", caption: "Sign in circled" }]}>

Type the email or username you use in the LedgerSync app. Click **Next**.

Type your password and click **Sign in**. Asked for a **Two Factor Authentication** code? Type the code from
your Google Authenticator app and click **Verify**.

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**The page says Create your LedgerSync account?** No LedgerSync login uses that email. Click **Change** and type
your LedgerSync username. Don't create a second account. Your firm has no LedgerSync account yet? Sign up at
[ledgersyncappv2.com/sign-up](https://ledgersyncappv2.com/sign-up) first, then come back here.

</AiNote>

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="allow" stage={4} n={4} total={5} title="Click Allow, then Allow access" imgs={[{ src: "/images/ai-connector/full/ls-5-allow.png", caption: "Allow circled" }, { src: "/images/ai-connector/full/claude-4-allow-access.png", caption: "Allow access circled" }]}>

You see your firm's name. Click **Allow**.

Then Claude asks to access your account. Click **Allow access**. You only see this the first time.

Claude opens again and says **Connected to LedgerSync**.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="always-allow" stage={4} n={5} total={5} title="Stop Claude asking permission every time" img="/images/ai-connector/full/claude-5-always-allow.png" caption="Always allow circled">

Claude now shows the LedgerSync page. Next to **Read-only tools**, click **Needs approval**, then click **Always
allow**.

Before Claude changes something, it still asks you first. Allow it if it looks right.

Came back later? Open [claude.ai/customize/connectors/yours](https://claude.ai/customize/connectors/yours) and
click **LedgerSync**.

</AiStep>

<AiTest text="Use LedgerSync to check whether my account is ready." stage={5}>

Click **Copy**. In Claude, start a new chat, click the message box, press **Ctrl+V** (Command+V on a Mac),
then press **Enter**.

</AiTest>

- Claude says your LedgerSync account is ready: you're done.
- Anything else: see [Troubleshooting](/ai/troubleshooting).
