New to LedgerSync? You create your account when you sign in, in the steps below.

You need ChatGPT Plus or higher. It works only on [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com) in a web browser, not
in the phone app.

<Note>
  **ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Edu?** Only your workspace admin can add LedgerSync. Ask them to add it
  for your workspace. Then open **Settings**, then **Plugins**, click **LedgerSync** and click **Connect**,
  and go to [step 5](#sign-in).
</Note>

**Your LedgerSync address.** You paste it in step 4. Click the copy icon on the right of the box.

```text
https://api.ledgersyncappv2.com/mcp
```

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Developer mode" id="developer-mode">
    Open [chatgpt.com/settings/security](https://chatgpt.com/settings/security). Or click **Settings**, then
    **Security and login**.

    Turn on **Developer mode**. Without it, **Create MCP App** does not appear in step 3.

    ChatGPT may show a risk warning. Turn it on anyway: LedgerSync needs it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Plugins, then Add" id="plugins">
    Open [chatgpt.com/plugins](https://chatgpt.com/plugins). Or click **Plugins** on the left. Then click
    **Add**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/chatgpt-plugins-add.png" alt="ChatGPT Plugins page with the Add button marked" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create MCP App" id="create-app">
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/chatgpt-create-mcp-app.png" alt="The Add menu with Create MCP App marked" />
    </Frame>

    Don't see **Create MCP App**? Check that **Developer mode** is on ([step 1](#developer-mode)), then come
    back. On ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Edu, only your admin can do this: ask them to add LedgerSync
    for everyone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the New Plugin form" id="form">
    1. In **Name**, type **LedgerSync**.
    2. Keep **Server URL**. Click the box below it, then press **Ctrl+V** (Windows) or **Command+V** (Mac)
       to paste the address.
    3. Keep **OAuth** under **Authentication**.
    4. Tick the box **I understand and want to continue**.
    5. Click **Create**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/chatgpt-new-plugin.png" alt="ChatGPT New Plugin form with LedgerSync, the address and OAuth filled in, and the I understand and want to continue box and the Create button marked" />
    </Frame>

    **About the warning box.** ChatGPT shows this warning for every app you add yourself. You see it because
    LedgerSync is not in ChatGPT's app list yet. Tick it and continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to LedgerSync" id="sign-in">
    The LedgerSync sign-in page opens. Type your email or your LedgerSync username. Click **Next**.
    Already signed in to LedgerSync in this browser? You go straight to the **Allow** page.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/connect-1-email.png" alt="The Sign in to LedgerSync page with the Next button marked" />
    </Frame>

    - **Your firm uses LedgerSync:** type your LedgerSync password. Click **Sign in**. You use two-step
      sign-in? Type your code and click **Verify**. See **Create your LedgerSync account** instead? Do not
      click it. Click **Change** and type your LedgerSync username.
    - **New to LedgerSync:** the page says **Create your LedgerSync account**. Click **Create account** and
      follow [New to LedgerSync](/ai/new-to-ledgersync). It ends on the **Allow** page below.
    - **Approved developer accounts:** type your developer portal email. LedgerSync sends you to the developer
      portal sign-in. An application still waiting for its first approval cannot connect yet.

    Then click **Allow**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/connect-5-allow.png" alt="The Connect your AI assistant page with the Allow button marked" />
    </Frame>

    ChatGPT asks to access your account. Click **Allow access**. You only see this the first time. The picture shows this page for Claude.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/ledgersync-allow-access.png" alt="LedgerSync page asking to let Claude access your account, with Allow access marked" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it in a new chat" id="test">
    Click **New chat**. Type **@** and pick **LedgerSync**. Then ask:

    ```text
    Use LedgerSync to check whether my account is ready.
    ```

    ChatGPT looks things up without asking, and asks before it changes anything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Now ask
