<Accordion title="On ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Edu? Open this first">

Only your workspace admin can add LedgerSync for your workspace. Adding it needs a LedgerSync sign-in: your admin
signs in with their own LedgerSync login. Your admin has no LedgerSync login?
[Book a call with our team](https://ledgersync.com/book-a-demo) and we'll set it up with you. Nobody creates a new
LedgerSync account.

Otherwise, send this page to your admin: the address is in step 3. They add it from **Workspace settings** and then
click **Publish**. Until then it is a draft that only they can use. Everyone in your workspace then sees LedgerSync.
Then each person connects it with their own LedgerSync login.

Once it is published, each person opens
[chatgpt.com/settings/plugins-settings](https://chatgpt.com/settings/plugins-settings) (**Settings**, then
**Plugins**), clicks **LedgerSync**, clicks **Connect**, and does steps 4 and 5 below, and the test.

</Accordion>

<AiStep id="developer-mode" stage={2} n={1} total={5} title="Turn on Developer mode, if you see it">

Open [chatgpt.com/settings/security](https://chatgpt.com/settings/security) (it opens in a new tab). That is
**Settings**, then **Security and login**. Turn on **Developer mode**. ChatGPT may show a risk warning. Turn it on
anyway: LedgerSync needs it. Then come back to this tab for step 2.

Don't see **Developer mode** there? Skip this step and go on to step 2.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="plugins" stage={2} n={2} total={5} title="Click Add, then Create MCP App" img="/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-2-create-mcp-app.png" caption="Create MCP App circled">

<AiButton href="https://chatgpt.com/plugins" newTab>Open ChatGPT Plugins</AiButton>

ChatGPT opens in a new tab. Keep it open, and come back to this tab for step 3. In ChatGPT, click **Add** at
the top right, then **Create MCP App**.

LedgerSync is already under **Settings**, then **Plugins**? Don't create it again:
[connect again](/ai/troubleshooting#connect-again) instead. Only under **Plugins**, then **Personal**? Click it, then
**Install plugin**.

Don't see **Create MCP App**? Turn on **Developer mode** (step 1). On ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Edu,
ask your workspace admin. Still not there? [Book a call with our team](https://ledgersync.com/book-a-demo) and
we'll set it up with you.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="form" stage={2} n={3} total={5} title="Fill in the form" img="/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-3-form.png" caption="Address box, tick box and Create circled">

1. In **Name**, type **LedgerSync**.
2. Click **Copy** below. Under **Connection**, click the long box that says `https://example.com/mcp` and press
   **Ctrl+V** (Windows) or **Command+V** (Mac).
3. Leave everything else as it is.
4. Tick **I understand and want to continue**.
5. Click **Create**.

<AiCopy label="Your LedgerSync address" text="https://api.ledgersyncappv2.com/mcp" />

<AiNote tone="yellow">

ChatGPT shows this warning for every app you add yourself, because LedgerSync is not in ChatGPT's list
yet. It's safe to tick.

</AiNote>

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="sign-in" stage={3} n={4} total={5} title="Sign in with your LedgerSync login" imgs={[{ src: "/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-4-continue.png", caption: "Continue to LedgerSync circled" }, { src: "/images/ai-connector/full/ls-1-email.png", caption: "Your email or username, then Next" }, { src: "/images/ai-connector/full/ls-2-password.png", caption: "Sign in circled" }]}>

ChatGPT shows **Connect LedgerSync**. Click **Continue to LedgerSync**. The LedgerSync sign-in page opens.

Type the email or username you use in the LedgerSync app. Click **Next**.

Type your password and click **Sign in**. Asked for a **Two Factor Authentication** code? Type the code from
your Google Authenticator app and click **Verify**.

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**The page says Create your LedgerSync account?** No LedgerSync login uses that email. Click **Change** and type
your LedgerSync username. Don't create a second account. Your firm has no LedgerSync account yet? Sign up at
[ledgersyncappv2.com/sign-up](https://ledgersyncappv2.com/sign-up) first, then come back here.

</AiNote>

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="allow" stage={4} n={5} total={5} title="Click Allow, then Allow access" imgs={[{ src: "/images/ai-connector/full/ls-5-allow.png", caption: "Allow circled" }, { src: "/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-5-allow-access.png", caption: "Allow access circled" }]}>

You see your firm's name. Click **Allow**.

Then ChatGPT asks to access your account. Click **Allow access**. You only see this the first time.

ChatGPT opens its Plugins page again.

</AiStep>

<AiTest text="Use LedgerSync to check whether my account is ready." stage={5}>

Click **Copy**. In ChatGPT, start a new chat (**New chat**, or **Home** at the top left). Type **@**, then
**Ledg**, and pick **LedgerSync**. Press **Ctrl+V** (Command+V on a Mac), then press **Enter**.

</AiTest>

- ChatGPT says your LedgerSync account is ready: you're done.
- LedgerSync is not in the list after **@**? Open [Plugins](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), click **Personal**, then
  **LedgerSync**, then **Install plugin**. Not there either? Do steps 2 to 5 again. On Business, Enterprise or
  Edu, ask your workspace admin.
- Anything else: see [Troubleshooting](/ai/troubleshooting).
