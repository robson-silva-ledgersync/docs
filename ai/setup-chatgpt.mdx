<AiProgress current={2} />

<AiStep id="developer-mode" n={1} total={5} title="Turn on Developer mode, if you see it">

Open [chatgpt.com/settings/security](https://chatgpt.com/settings/security) (**Settings**, then **Security and
login**) and turn on **Developer mode**. ChatGPT may show a risk warning. Turn it on anyway: LedgerSync needs it.

Don't see **Developer mode** there? Skip this step and go on to step 2.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="plugins" n={2} total={5} title="Click Add, then Create MCP App" img="/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-2-create-mcp-app.png" caption="Create MCP App circled">

<AiButton href="https://chatgpt.com/plugins" newTab>Open ChatGPT Plugins</AiButton>

ChatGPT opens in a new tab. Keep it open, and come back to this tab for step 3. In ChatGPT, click **Add** at
the top right, then **Create MCP App**.

LedgerSync is already under **Settings**, then **Plugins**? Don't create it again:
[connect again](/ai/troubleshooting#connect-again) instead. Only under **Plugins**, then **Personal**? Click it, then
**Install plugin**.

Don't see **Create MCP App**? Turn on **Developer mode** (step 1). On ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Edu,
ask your workspace admin. Still not there? [Book a call with our team](https://ledgersync.com/book-a-demo) and
we'll set it up with you.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="form" n={3} total={5} title="Fill in the form" img="/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-3-form.png" caption="Address box, tick box and Create circled">

1. In **Name**, type **LedgerSync**.
2. Click **Copy** below. Under **Connection**, click the long box that says `https://example.com/mcp` and press
   **Ctrl+V** (Windows) or **Command+V** (Mac).
3. Leave everything else as it is.
4. Tick **I understand and want to continue**.
5. Click **Create**.

<AiCopy label="Your LedgerSync address" text="https://api.ledgersyncappv2.com/mcp" />

<AiNote tone="yellow">

ChatGPT shows this warning for every app you add yourself, because LedgerSync is not in ChatGPT's list
yet. It's safe to tick.

</AiNote>

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="sign-in" n={4} total={5} title="Sign in, or create your account" imgs={[{ src: "/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-4-continue.png", caption: "Continue to LedgerSync circled" }, { src: "/images/ai-connector/full/ls-1-email.png", caption: "Next circled" }, { src: "/images/ai-connector/full/ls-2-password.png", caption: "Sign in circled" }]}>

ChatGPT shows **Connect LedgerSync**. Click **Continue to LedgerSync**. The LedgerSync sign-in page opens.

Type the email or username you use in the LedgerSync app. New to LedgerSync? Type your work email. Click
**Next**.

Your firm already uses LedgerSync: type your password and click **Sign in**. Asked for a **Two Factor
Authentication** code? Type the code from your Google Authenticator app and click **Verify**.

New to LedgerSync: create your account with steps 4a to 4d, just below.

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**Your firm already uses LedgerSync?** If the page says **Create your LedgerSync account**, click **Change** and
type your LedgerSync username instead.

</AiNote>

</AiStep>

<Accordion title="New to LedgerSync? Create your account here (4 short steps)">

<AiStep id="allow" n={5} total={5} title="Click Allow, then Allow access" imgs={[{ src: "/images/ai-connector/full/ls-5-allow.png", caption: "Allow circled" }, { src: "/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-5-allow-access.png", caption: "Allow access circled" }]}>

You see your firm's name. Click **Allow**.

Then ChatGPT asks to access your account. Click **Allow access**. You only see this the first time.

ChatGPT opens its Plugins page again.

</AiStep>

<AiTest text="Use LedgerSync to check whether my account is ready.">

Click **Copy**. In ChatGPT, start a new chat (**New chat**, or **Home** at the top left). Type **@**, then
**Ledg**, and pick **LedgerSync**. Press **Ctrl+V** (Command+V on a Mac), then press **Enter**.

</AiTest>

- ChatGPT says your LedgerSync account is ready: you're done.
- It says **We're reviewing your new firm**: add your clients now. Ask: "Add my client" and the client's name.
  Bank connections open after the review.
- LedgerSync is not in the list after **@**? Open [Plugins](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), click **Personal**, then
  **LedgerSync**, then **Install plugin**. Not there either? Do steps 2 to 5 again.
- Anything else: see [Troubleshooting](/ai/troubleshooting).
