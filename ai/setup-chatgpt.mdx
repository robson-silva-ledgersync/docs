<AiStep id="developer-mode" n={1} total={5} title="Turn on Developer mode">

Open **Settings**, then **Security and login**, and turn on **Developer mode**. ChatGPT may show a risk
warning. Turn it on anyway: LedgerSync needs it.

Don't see **Developer mode** there? Go on to step 2. If **Create MCP App** is in the menu, you're set.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="plugins" n={2} total={5} title="Click Plugins, then Add, then Create MCP App" img="/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-2-create-mcp-app.png" caption="Create MCP App circled">

Open [chatgpt.com/plugins](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), or click **Plugins** on the left. Click **Add**,
then **Create MCP App**.

Don't see **Create MCP App**? Turn on **Developer mode** (step 1). On ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Edu,
ask your workspace admin.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="form" n={3} total={5} title="Fill in the form" img="/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-3-form.png" caption="Warning box and Create circled">

1. In **Name**, type **LedgerSync**.
2. Under **Connection**, keep **Server URL**. Click the box below it and paste the address: **Ctrl+V**
   (Windows) or **Command+V** (Mac).
3. Keep **OAuth** under **Authentication**.
4. Tick **I understand and want to continue**.
5. Click **Create**.

<AiNote tone="yellow">

ChatGPT shows this warning for every app you add yourself, because LedgerSync is not in ChatGPT's list
yet. It's safe to tick.

</AiNote>

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="sign-in" n={4} total={5} title="Sign in, then click Allow" img="/images/ai-connector/full/ls-1-email.png" caption="The LedgerSync sign-in page">

The LedgerSync sign-in page opens. Sign in with your LedgerSync email or username, and your password. Click
**Allow**. Then ChatGPT asks to access your account: click **Allow access**. You only see this the first
time.

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**New to LedgerSync?** You create your account on this sign-in page. See
[New to LedgerSync](/ai/new-to-ledgersync).

</AiNote>

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="new-chat" n={5} total={5} title="Open a new chat">

Click **New chat**. Type **@** and pick **LedgerSync**. ChatGPT looks things up without asking, and asks
before it changes anything.

</AiStep>
