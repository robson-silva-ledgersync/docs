<AiPage title="Turn a bank statement PDF into transactions" subtitle="LedgerSync reads the PDF and shows the transactions in your chat. It can also make a CSV (spreadsheet), Excel or QuickBooks file. Nothing is added to a client.">

<AiNote tone="gray">

**Don't attach the PDF to the chat.** The AI cannot pass a file to LedgerSync. It gives you a LedgerSync page
to upload it instead.

</AiNote>

<AiStep id="ask" n={1} total={5} title="Ask the AI">

Ask: *"Convert a bank statement PDF into transactions."* The AI gives you a link to a LedgerSync page.

</AiStep>

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**The AI does not know how to do this?** Your AI still has an older copy of LedgerSync's tools. Do
[After a LedgerSync update](/ai/troubleshooting#after-an-update), then start a new chat and ask again.

</AiNote>

<AiStep id="open" n={2} total={5} title="Open the link">

Click the link the AI gave you. The page says **Upload your bank statement**.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="choose" n={3} total={5} title="Choose the PDF" img="/images/ai-convert/upload-1-choose.png" caption="Choose PDF">

Click **Choose PDF** and pick the statement on your computer or phone.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="upload" n={4} total={5} title="Click Upload" imgs={[{ src: "/images/ai-convert/upload-2-confirm.png", caption: "Upload" }, { src: "/images/ai-convert/upload-3-done.png", caption: "Done" }]}>

The page shows the file's name. Click **Upload**. Wait until the page says **Done**.

Picked the wrong file? Click **Choose a different file** before you click **Upload**.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="done" n={5} total={5} title="Tell the AI you are done">

Go back to your chat and tell the AI: *"Done."* It shows the transactions. A long statement? The AI may offer a
file instead of listing every line.

Still reading? Wait a minute, then say *"Done."* again.

</AiStep>

<AiNote tone="gray">

**Want a file?** Ask: *"Give me an Excel file."* Or a CSV, or a QuickBooks file. The AI gives you a download
link. It works once, for 10 minutes. Link not working? Ask the AI for a new one.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="green">

**The AI tells you when the totals can't be checked.** If the transactions don't match the statement's
balances, or the balances could not be read, check them before you use them.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="gray">

**One link, one statement.** Each link takes one PDF and works for 24 hours. After 3 failed uploads it stops
working. For another statement, or a new try, ask again.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="gray">

**It is also in the LedgerSync app.** The conversion shows on your **PDF Converter** page, like one you made
there. If you delete it there, the AI can no longer show it.

</AiNote>

Something went wrong? See [Troubleshooting](/ai/troubleshooting).

<Accordion title="For developers: tools, files and limits">
  - Tools: `ledgersync_create_statement_upload_link` makes the upload page link and a `conversion_id`
    (`cnv_...`). `ledgersync_get_statement_conversion` says `waiting_for_upload`, `in_progress`, `completed`
    (with the transactions, a page at a time), `failed`, `expired` or `deleted` (removed on the **PDF Converter**
    page). `ledgersync_get_conversion_download_link`
    makes a one-time link to the file: `csv`, `xlsx`, `qbo` or `ofx`.
  - It is the same reader as the PDF Converter in the LedgerSync app and `POST /v3/statements/extract`.
  - Each person's conversions are their own: the owner, administrators and employees each see only the ones
    they made. A read-only connection can read conversions but cannot upload.
  - Limits: your firm can convert 3 statements at a time and 500 a day on live (2 and 50 on sandbox), shared
    with your API keys. Each AI app can make 30 upload links an hour. PDFs can be up to 30 MB.
</Accordion>
