The firm owner does this, once. You create your LedgerSync account while you set up your AI. It takes a few
minutes.

<Warning>
  **Does your firm already use the LedgerSync app?** Do not create a new account and do not pay. Go to
  [Firm already uses LedgerSync](/ai/existing-customers) instead.
</Warning>

Prices are on [ledgersync.com/pricing](https://ledgersync.com/pricing).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add LedgerSync to your AI">
    Follow [Set up in Claude](/ai/setup-claude) or [Set up in ChatGPT](/ai/setup-chatgpt). When the
    LedgerSync sign-in page opens, do the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your work email">
    Type your work email. Click **Next**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/connect-1-email.png" alt="The Sign in to LedgerSync page with the Next button marked" />
    </Frame>

    Use your work email, not a free email like Gmail. Do not use an email with a plus sign (+).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create account">
    The page says **Create your LedgerSync account**. Click **Create account**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/connect-3-create-account.png" alt="The Create your LedgerSync account page with the Create account button marked" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    Your email is already there. Type your name, your phones and your firm's name. Type a password twice:
    10 or more characters, with a capital letter, a small letter, a number and a symbol. Click **NEXT**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/connect-4-sign-up-form.png" alt="The Accountant registration form filled in, with the NEXT button marked" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your card">
    Check your order. Type your card details. Click **Subscribe**.

    This page is from Zoho, our billing partner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait one moment">
    The page says **Creating your account**. It may then say **Getting your account ready**. Do not close
    it. It moves on by itself. If it says **Wait 2 minutes, then click Try again**, do that.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Allow">
    Click **Allow**. Then your AI asks to access your account: click **Allow access**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-connector/connect-5-allow.png" alt="The Connect your AI assistant page with the Allow button marked" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

We check every new firm by hand. Until then, your AI says: **We're reviewing your new firm. You can add
clients now. Bank connections open after the review.**

You can add your clients now. Bank connections open after the review.

Stuck? Email [support@ledgersync.com](mailto:support@ledgersync.com) with the step and what the screen says.
Never send passwords or card numbers.
