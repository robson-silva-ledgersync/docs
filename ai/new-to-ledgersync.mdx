<AiPage title="New to LedgerSync: create your account" subtitle="The firm owner does this, once. It takes a few minutes, right inside your AI's sign-in.">

<AiProgress current={3} />

<AiNote tone="green">

**Start in your AI.** Follow [Set up in Claude](/ai/setup-claude) or [Set up in ChatGPT](/ai/setup-chatgpt).
When the LedgerSync sign-in page opens, do these 5 steps. Prices are on
[ledgersync.com/pricing](https://ledgersync.com/pricing).

</AiNote>

<AiStep id="email" n={1} total={5} title="Type your work email" img="/images/ai-connector/full/ls-1-email.png" caption="Next circled">

Type your work email. Click **Next**.

Use your work email, not a free email like Gmail. Don't use an email with a plus sign (+).

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="create" n={2} total={5} title="Click Create account" img="/images/ai-connector/full/ls-3-create-account.png" caption="Create account circled">

The page says **Create your LedgerSync account**. Click **Create account**.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="form" n={3} total={5} title="Fill in the form" img="/images/ai-connector/full/ls-4-sign-up-form.png" caption="NEXT circled">

Your email is already there. Type your name, your phones and your firm's name.

Type a password twice: 10 or more characters, with a capital letter, a small letter, a number and a symbol.
Click **NEXT**.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="card" n={4} total={5} title="Add your card">

Check your order. Type your card details. Click **Subscribe**.

This page is from Zoho, our billing partner.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="allow" n={5} total={5} title="Click Allow" img="/images/ai-connector/full/ls-5-allow.png" caption="Allow circled">

The page says **Creating your account**. It may then say **Getting your account ready**. Don't close it: it
moves on by itself. If it says **Wait 2 minutes, then click Try again**, do that.

Then click **Allow**. Your AI asks to access your account: click **Allow access**.

</AiStep>

<AiBox title="What happens next">

We check every new firm by hand. Until then, your AI says: **We're reviewing your new firm. You can add
clients now. Bank connections open after the review.**

You can add your clients now. Bank connections open after the review.

</AiBox>
