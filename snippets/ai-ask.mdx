### Add a client and connect their bank

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your client">
    Ask: "Add my client Acme."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect their bank">
    Ask: "Send Acme a link to connect their bank." Open the link and pick your client's bank on the LedgerSync
    page. Then sign in with their bank login on the secure page that opens: LedgerSync's own, or our bank-data
    partner's for some banks. Never in the chat.

    Don't have their bank login? Send the link to your client by email or text, and they do it. The link
    works for 3 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask about their bank">
    When the bank is connected, ask: "Show Acme's bank balances."
  </Step>
</Steps>

send a normal link too if you also want the bank's transactions.
